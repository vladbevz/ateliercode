## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are small local businesses and independent professionals in Nîmes and the Gard department (Occitanie, France): artisans and shopkeepers (plumbers, bakers, hairdressers), professions libérales (doctors, lawyers, consultants, coaches), PME/TPE wanting a product catalogue or online store, and independent creators (photographers, personal trainers, instructors). They need a site that actually brings in clients through local search, not a digital brochure nobody finds.

## Product Purpose

AtelierCode builds custom websites — site vitrine, e-commerce, or bespoke web applications — for small businesses, delivered fast (7–10 days for a vitrine, 2–4 weeks for e-commerce) and structured to be found on Google from day one. Success means the client's site converts visitors into calls, visits, or sales — not just looking good.

## Positioning

The mechanism a neighboring agency could not truthfully copy: most French web agencies charge around 2 000 € for sites built on heavy, generic stacks (WordPress, Elementor, templated CMS) that end up slow, visually generic/dated, and poorly adapted to what the local market actually needs. AtelierCode hand-codes every site in Next.js/React/TypeScript instead — no CMS, no plugin bloat — delivering genuinely fast sites (Lighthouse 95+, LCP < 2.5s) at a lower, transparent price, with the founder-developer as the one point of contact throughout, not a sales intermediary.

## Operating Context

Clients reach out via the contact form, phone, or WhatsApp; the first meeting can happen in person in Nîmes or remotely. Development follows a four-stage process communicated transparently so the client always knows where their project stands: Analyse & Stratégie (1–2j), Design & UX (3–4j), Développement (5–7j), Lancement & Suivi (1–2j). Payment is 50% upfront / 50% on delivery, via bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Next.js 14 (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion (used selectively — Header, CustomCursor, ScrollToTop, WhatsAppButton — not as the default mechanism for page-content reveal animation, which uses a lightweight dependency-free IntersectionObserver component specifically to avoid per-route bundle growth). Hosted on Vercel.
- Explicitly does not build on WordPress, Elementor, Webflow/Bubble (no-code tools), or Magento — stated as a differentiator on the site, not a gap to fill.
- Does not publish native iOS/Android apps; "application web" offerings are browser-based.
- Micro-entreprise status: no VAT charged (art. 293 B du CGI).
- Pricing: Site Vitrine dès 799 €, E-commerce dès 1290 €, Application web sur devis.
- AI agents / AI transformation consulting is explicitly **not** offered yet. Noted only as a possible future service — out of scope, and must not be implied or advertised anywhere on the site.

## Brand Commitments

Name: AtelierCode. Single-founder identity — Vladyslav Bevz, full-stack developer based in Nîmes — is a core, literal promise: the person who answers, designs, builds, tests, and delivers the site is always the same person, with no sales layer in between.

## Evidence on Hand

Real delivered client projects are documented in `app/lib/projects-data.ts` and shown on `/realisations`, including Le 438 (restaurant, Vauvert — genuine +36% reservations since launch), Lymar Dermo Esthetic, KFM Transport (fleet-management dashboard), and others. No fabricated testimonials, client logos, or stats exist beyond these real, named projects — future work must not invent client names, numbers, or case studies.

## Product Principles

1. Speed is the proven, non-negotiable competitive edge (Lighthouse 95+, LCP < 2.5s) — no visual or feature addition may regress it.
2. One point of contact, the developer, from first message to delivery.
3. Hand-written code over CMS/templates/no-code tools — a stated differentiator, not an implementation detail to hide.
4. Transparent, flat pricing; no recurring subscriptions or hidden fees.
5. Local-first SEO and copy: content targets real Nîmes/Gard search intent, not generic national boilerplate.
